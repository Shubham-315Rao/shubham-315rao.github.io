About the game

Caramel Crushers is a match-3 puzzle game. Swap neighbouring candies to line up three or more of the same kind, reach each level's goal before you run out of moves or time, and travel along the map to the next level.

  • Thousands of levels that get trickier as you go.
  • Boosters for tough spots, and lives that refill over time.
  • A daily bonus and a spin wheel with free coins and rewards.
  • Invite friends with your own code and earn coins when they join.

Ads and purchases

The game is free to play. It shows rewarded videos only: you watch one only when you choose to, in exchange for a reward such as unlocking more levels, extra moves, coins, a booster, a life or an extra spin. There are no banner ads and no forced ads.

Coin packs are available as optional in-app purchases.

Saving your progress and signing in

You can play the whole game without signing in, and your progress is saved automatically. Signing in is optional: it keeps your progress and coins safe when you change or lose your phone. On Android you can sign in with Google Play Games or Google; on iPhone and iPad, with Apple or Google.

If you sign in with Google, Caramel Crushers receives your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture link. They are used only to sign you in, to show which account you are using, and to save your progress. They are never used for advertising and never sold. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

You can delete your account at any time in the game (tap the @ button, then “Delete account”) or by following these instructions.
